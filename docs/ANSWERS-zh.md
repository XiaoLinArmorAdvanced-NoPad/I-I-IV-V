# 视听说课后作业2 —— 逐题严格推导（第二次修订）

**方法**：每题找**唯一性原文锚点**，并检查每个错误选项"错在哪"。
若某题有两个选项都能被原文支持，说明转写有误或选项理解有偏差，需要额外验证。

---

## 题组 1~4：飞机上聊读书

**Q1. What does the woman say about light reads?**
- A) They are interesting but tiring to read.
- B) They are relaxing but not engaging enough.
- C) They are short without much tension in the plot.
- D) They are easy to read without much mental effort.

**原文**：女人说 "I usually lean toward light reads, you know, books that you can **flip through quickly** without too much **mental strain**. Mysteries and romance novels are my favorites. They are **easy to follow** and they keep me **relaxed yet engaged**."

**推导**：
- D：`easy to follow` = `easy to read` ✅；`without too much mental strain` = `without much mental effort` ✅ → **两处同义替换，D 必选**
- A：错。"interesting" 是**男人**听完后敷衍的那句 "Interesting"；"tiring" 无出处。
- B：错。她说的是 "relaxed yet **engaged**"（放松**且**投入），B 说 "not engaging enough" 正好反了。
- C：错。`flip through quickly` 指她翻得快，不是"书篇幅短"；`tension in the plot` 无出处。

**答案：D**（高置信）

---

**Q2. Why does the man like reading nonfiction on a flight?**
- A) He likes the feel of learning at a high altitude.
- B) He enjoys the mental escape to different places.
- C) He likes discussing how to read nonfiction.
- D) Reading about the real world helps him interact with people.

**原文**：男人说 "I tend to go for non-fiction. I prefer **biographies, travelogues** or even science books while flying. There's something about **learning while cruising at 30,000 feet** that feels oddly satisfying." 女人接："learning about **real people** or **exploring new places** can be fascinating."

**推导**：
- A：`learning` + `at a high altitude`(=30,000 feet) → 表面高度吻合 ⚠️
- B：`mental escape` + `different places`(=exploring new places / travelogues / biographies) → 语义层吻合 ⚠️
- C：完全无出处（这句话在对话里不存在）→ 排除
- D：`interact with people` 无出处 → 排除

**⚠️ 这题是本次最大的不确定点。** A 与 B 都有原文支撑：
- 若考"字面复现" → A
- 若考"同义概括" → B

**我的判断：A**（`the feel of learning at a high altitude` 与 `feeling oddly satisfying while learning at 30,000 feet` 是**唯一一处作者特意强调"感觉/感受"的表述**，出题人通常围绕它设题；B 的 "escape" 原文是**女人**说她自己的状态 "I just want to escape"）

---

**Q3. What does the woman plan to read?**
- A) A travel memoir.  B) A romance story.  C) A mystery novel.  D) A biographic book.

**原文**：男人问 "what's on your **reading list** for this flight?"（→ 对应题干 `plan to read`）；女人答 "I've got a **mystery novel** in my bag — a missing diamond, a smart detective and a twisty plot."

**推导**：`plan to read` = `reading list`，答案原词命中。
- A 是**男人**带的（travel memoir about hiking the Himalayas）→ 张冠李戴陷阱
- B 是她喜欢的**类型**，不是这次带的书
- D 无出处

**答案：C**（高置信）

---

**Q4. Where does this conversation take place?**
- A) On a boat.  B) On a plane.  C) At a bookstore.  D) At a reading club.

**原文**："we've **boarded**"、"**flying** a bit nerve-racking"、"while **flying at a high altitude**"、"the **flight** feel shorter"、"cruising at 30,000 feet"

**推导**：五处独立证据全部指向飞机。
**答案：B**（高置信）

## 题组 5~8：母子谈数学作业

**Q5. What is bothering the boy?**
- A) He is having difficulties with math.
- B) His dad will be home late for dinner.
- C) His parents plan to talk to his teacher.
- D) He doesn't have time to do math homework.

**原文**：男孩说 "this math homework is taking forever. I **don't like math**. It's just **too hard for me**." 妈妈说 "your dad and I **will talk to your teacher**"，男孩回 "**No, please don't**."

**推导**：
- A：`too hard for me` + `taking forever` = `having difficulties with math` ✅ 直接对应他的抱怨
- C：`will talk to your teacher` 是**妈妈的决定**；男孩说 "No, please don't" 说明这让他**更烦**，但它不是最初的困扰 ⚠️
- B：dinner 时间只是开场白，不是困扰
- D：**语法就排除了**——他没有"没时间"，是"做不完+觉得难"

**⚠️ 次不确定点。** A 与 C 都可辩：
- 男孩的核心抱怨是"数学太难" → A
- 但题干 `bothering`（烦扰）也可指让他抵触的那件事（父母要找老师）→ C

**我的判断：A**（`too hard for me` 是唯一的直接情绪表达）

**Q6. What do we learn about Hua Luogeng (转写作 "Xin Jing run")?**
- A) He grew up in a family with financial stability.
- B) He travelled a lot after college for math research.
- C) He wasn't known for his research during his lifetime.
- D) He wasn't successful in his job as a high school teacher.

**原文**："he grew up in a **poor family** and **worked as a high school teacher after college**, but **teaching wasn't considered his strength**."

**推导**：
- D：`teaching wasn't considered his strength` = `wasn't successful as a high school teacher` ✅ 原词级对应
- A：`poor family` 与 `financial stability` **正相反** → 反向陷阱
- B：原文是"住小房间、日夜研究"，**没有**"四处旅行" → 无出处
- C：原文 "he was a **famous** Chinese mathematician"、"achieved **greatness**" → 与 C 相反

**答案：D**（高置信）

**Q7. Why does the woman tell the story of Hua Luogeng?**
- A) To teach the boy some good methods to learn math.
- B) To encourage the boy to study hard and be confident.
- C) To give an example of famous scientists' study habits.
- D) To explain the importance of studying math in school.

**原文**：她说 "if you stay **determined** and **keep working hard**, **you'll improve**. You know, even great mathematicians face difficulties." 结尾 "And **I believe you can do the same**."

**推导**：
- B：`keep working hard` + `I believe you can do the same`（=鼓励+信心）✅ 两项都命中
- A：没教任何"方法" → 无出处
- C：讲的是**励志经历**（贫穷、苦熬），不是"学习习惯" → 偏
- D：没讨论"在学校学数学的重要性" → 无出处

**答案：B**（高置信）

---

**Q8. What can we learn about the boy from the end of the conversation?**
- A) He realizes the importance of studying math.
- B) He will learn more about the history of math.
- C) He understands the importance of hard work.
- D) He wants to be a mathematician in the future.

**原文**：男孩结尾说 "Wow, that's inspiring. I guess **being smart isn't enough. You also need to work hard** to do well in math."

**推导**：
- C：`You also need to work hard` = `the importance of hard work` ✅ 几乎原句
- A：他领悟的是"努力"而非"学数学的重要性" → 偏
- B：无出处
- D：无出处

**答案：C**（高置信）

## 题组 9~12：警局报案

**Q9. What happened to the woman?**
- A) Her car was stolen while she was having a party.
- B) She lost the birthday gifts her friends bought her.
- C) Many of her personal details were exposed to theft.
- D) Someone broke into her car and took away her stuff.

**原文**："when I came outside this morning to go to work, I found **my car broken into and my belongings were stolen**."

**推导**：
- D：`car broken into` + `belongings were stolen` ✅ **字面完全对应**
- A：车是**被撬**不是被偷走；派对发生在**昨天**，且她在屋里睡觉，不是"开派对时车被偷" → 双重错误
- B：失物是**妈妈**送的书（生日礼物）和**自己的**手机，不是"朋友送的礼物" → 主语错误
- C：`contact information` 是**警察登记流程**，不是"资料被泄露" → 措辞陷阱

**答案：D**（高置信）

---

**Q10. What does the man ask the woman to do about her incident?**
- A) Write a report about what happened to her.
- B) Calm down and not to make any complaints.
- C) File a report with serial numbers of lost items.
- D) Wait for the lost items to return to her someday.

**原文**："**please sign this form here**"、"Do you happen to have the **serial numbers** for your e-reader and phone? If you do **include them in the report**."

**推导**：
- C：`file a report` + `serial numbers` ✅ 两个关键点都命中
- A：只说"report"，**漏了 serial numbers**这个核心要求 → 不完整
- B：警察说 "I understand your frustration. But let's focus on gathering information" —— 是**安抚后继续办事**，不是"让你别投诉" → 曲解
- D：无出处

**答案：C**（高置信）

---

**Q11. Where does the conversation most probably take place?**
- A) Over the phone.  B) At a police station.  C) Near the woman's workplace.  D) At the woman's birthday party.

**原文**："**please sign this form here**"、"car break-ins are quite common here in **Los Angeles**"、"we'll do our best to **follow up** on this"

**推导**：
- B：当面签字 + 提交序列号 + 正式报案流程 ✅
- A：要**签字**就不可能是电话 ⚠️（唯一能挑战 B 的选项，但"sign this form"排除）
- C：她早上出门上班**发现**车被撬，但对话不在那里
- D：派对是昨天的事

**答案：B**（较高置信）

---

**Q12. What is the man's overall tone in speaking to the woman?**
- A) Cautious.  B) Indifferent.  C) Apologetic.  D) Matter-of-fact.

**原文**："I understand your frustration, ma'am. But for now, **let's focus on gathering all the necessary information**." / "car break-ins are quite common here... the chances of recovering are **quite slim**."

**推导**：
- D：冷静陈述事实、按流程办事、不掺杂情绪 ✅
- A：`cautious` = 谨慎小心，他不是在规避风险
- B：他说 "**I'm sorry** to say that..."、"I'm sorry again for what happened" → 不是冷漠 → 排除
- C：`apologetic` 有一定表面依据（他说了两次 sorry）⚠️ 但那是**程序性致歉**，全篇主导语气是"就事论事"

**答案：D**（较高置信）

## 置信度分级

| 置信度 | 题号 | 说明 |
|---|---|---|
| **高**（原文有字面/同义硬锚点） | 1, 3, 4, 6, 7, 8, 9, 10 | 8 题，几乎不可能错 |

| **存疑**（两个选项都能辩） | **2, 5** | 见下 |

## 两处存疑的详细说明

**第 2 题：A 还是 B？**
- A `the feel of learning at a high altitude` ← 对应男人的 "there's something about learning while cruising at 30,000 feet that feels oddly satisfying"
- B `enjoys the mental escape to different places` ← 对应 "exploring new places"，但 "escape" 一词原文是女人描述**她自己**的
- 我选 **A**。理由：出题人围绕男人那句带 `feels` 的自我陈述设题，A 是它的同义改写；B 混入了女人的立场。

**第 5 题：A 还是 C？**
- A `having difficulties with math` ← 对应 "too hard for me"、"taking forever"
- C `His parents plan to talk to his teacher` ← 对应 "your dad and I will talk to your teacher"，且男孩明确 "No, please don't"
- 我选 **A**。理由：题干问 `bothering`（困扰），他最初的、反复表达的是"数学太难"；C 是妈妈的提议（且他拒绝了），是对话中的**次生冲突**。
